You can check this hacky experiment out [here](https://www.shortsgenerator.com).

I'm really excited about this. The idea is to take a concept and create a full video using AI in minutes. There are various steps to get there

1\. Generate a script - either paste in your own or generate it by giving a prompt.

2\. Generate the audio - generate it using one of the AI voices or simply upload an Audio (skipping step 1).

3\. Generate the scenes - the AI will generate scenes based on the script. You can customize the images, durations, and visual effects.

4\. Stitch all of it together into an engaging video - voila you have your video!

There were lots of fun technical challenges to get all this to work and I am excited to see where this goes as the tech improves.
