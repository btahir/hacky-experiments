You can check this hacky experiment out [here](https://lexverse.vercel.app/).

The new OpenAI ada Embeddings had just come out and I wanted to see if I could build a Semantic Search app using them. Ended up [doing this for Lex Fridman's Youtube channel](https://twitter.com/deepwhitman/status/1610216151078047746).

I actually ended up using a different open source embeddings model but it was still fun. I also learned fly.io since I had to deploy the bulky model using [Python](https://www.sbert.net/).

Lexverse lets you do smart search on Lex Fridman's podcast videos and gives you back the exact points in time of a long interview something related to your query was brought up.

You can check this hacky experiment out [here](https://lexverse.vercel.app/).
