This was a fun Christmas project with my brother.

We created you-tldr because we spend hours every day watching YouTube videos. Inevitably, this sometimes involves watching videos which just did not warrant the time or effort.&#x20;

With you-tldr, you can effortlessly Summarize, Download, Search, and Interact with YouTube Videos in your language.

Because of the cool new developments in AI, we decided to completely revamp this project and build it out with the latest and greatest tech in March 2023. We made a lot of the previous Pro features free and just made the whole thing much better. Check it out. :)
