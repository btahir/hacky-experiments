You can check this hacky experiment out [here](https://www.mailtap.xyz/).

Mailtap was a fun project I built with the help of a friend I met at a Startup event (very random and spontaneous - just the way I like it). We thought it would be cool to have a simple QR Code generator that lets you start an email exchange with someone by instantly opening up an email with a greeting and your address populated.&#xA;&#xA;

We thought this was a huge time saver to exchange information as well as a way to keep the conversation going with your new connection! We added text messaging and LinkedIn options as well.

You can check this hacky experiment out [here](https://www.mailtap.xyz/).
