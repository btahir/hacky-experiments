You can check this hacky experiment out [here](https://www.plainscribe.com).

Taking a different path than YOU-TLDR, PlainScribe focuses on your own media files. Effortlessly transcribe your media files, break language barriers with translations, and distill key insights through summarization.

Upload your audio and video files up to 250MB without needing to worry about any limits. We take care of processing it and send you an email when its done.

It also does not lock you into any subscription. Only pay for what you use, based on the number of hours of audio/video transcribed or translated.
