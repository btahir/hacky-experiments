I wanted to see if I can leverage Notion to build a guide for better story writing. Writers like George Lucas and Dan Harmon use the Heroes Journey in their stories.&#xA;&#xA;This Notion template has everything setup for you so you can dive right in and start outlining your story based on the different stages of the Heroes Journey.&#xA;&#xA;**Here is a high level structure of this Template:**&#xA;&#xA;\- The Landing Page. It contains the links to all other pages and gives a snapshot summary of where everything is.&#xA;&#xA;\- Journey Template - The 12 Step guide.&#xA;&#xA;\- Book - This contains your actual book and the Chapters under the Heroes Journey Tool. You can either write the full chapters or use this space to fill out summaries and outlines of those chapters.&#xA;&#xA;\- Characters - A page where you can add information about your characters: their back story etc.&#xA;&#xA;\- Research - A section for adding research notes, links, todos.&#xA;
