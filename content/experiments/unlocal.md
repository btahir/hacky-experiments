You can check this hacky experiment out [here](https://www.unlocal.xyz/).

The genesis of this experiment came about because of a [Tweet](https://twitter.com/deepwhitman/status/1442548378156470273). 😀

&#x20;I wanted to build a simple and easy to use tool to track different time zones. I'm sure this is something a lot of us (myself included) have to do now as more companies and teams become globally distributed.

I used to call this The Clock App but then I added in Weather and am now thinking of adding more features so I have changed it to the weird and, hopefully catchy, name of Unlocal. 😀

The data is stored on your browser's local storage and you can even bookmark the site on your phone (since its a PWA) for a more 'app like' experience.

You can check this hacky experiment out [here](https://www.unlocal.xyz/).
