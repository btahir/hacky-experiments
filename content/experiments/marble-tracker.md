You can check this hacky experiment out [here](https://marbletracker.vercel.app/).

This app was inspired by something I read in James Clear's book Atomic Habits. You can also find details about this technique on his [blog](https://jamesclear.com/paper-clips). The idea is simple. Select the number of tasks/marbles you want to do and move them over as you complete the tasks. The visual cue of moving the marbles over motivates you to get through your list.&#xA;&#xA;Please note all data is stored locally on your browser. This is how the refresh state as well as 7 day history is maintained. This also means, for now, your data might change if you switch browsers or devices.
