You can check this hacky experiment out [here](https://www.bilal.news/).

This was a super fun project. I documented how I built this [here](https://twitter.com/deepwhitman/status/1633271493139517440).

The idea was to build my own personal Hacker News site but also make it easy for me to keep it up to date. I ended up building a chrome extension based on the web app.&#x20;

I read a ton of articles so this will be insightful to see any trends (added some analytics using Tremor.so to help with this). I even setup a newsletter so you can get a daily email with all the links from the past 24 hours. :D

And of course it can't be a Hacker News inspired site without RSS so that's there too. ;)
